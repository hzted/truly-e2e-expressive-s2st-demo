Truly End-to-End Expressive Speech-to-Speech Translation via Voice Conversion for Expressive Multilingual Alignmen


Anonymous Authors

Abstract: Expressive speech-to-speech translation (S2ST) remains limited by the lack of multilingual speech pairs that are parallel not only in con- tent but also in speaker identity and prosody. Existing systems therefore often rely on mod- ular training, target-side prompts, or partially aligned supervision, creating a mismatch be- tween training and inference. Dubbed speech provides a promising starting point because it naturally exhibits substantial temporal and prosodic correspondence across languages, but cross-lingual speaker mismatch makes it less suitable for truly end-to-end training, where the translation pairs are parallel in every as- pect. We propose a voice-conversion-based data construction framework that reduces this mismatch and turns dubbed speech into training pairs better tailored for direct expressive S2ST. To validate our data-level strategy, we train a direct S2ST model that maps source speech to target speech units without target-side acous- tic prompts or intermediate expressive-transfer modules. Together, the converted speech pairs and direct model provide a more train-test- consistent setup for truly end-to-end expressive S2ST.
