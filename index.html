Truly End-to-End Expressive Speech-to-Speech Translation via Voice Conversion for Expressive Multilingual Alignment

Anonymous Authors

Listen to Samples View Framework
Abstract: Expressive speech-to-speech translation (S2ST) remains limited by the lack of multilingual speech pairs that are parallel not only in content but also in speaker identity and prosody. Existing systems therefore often rely on modular training, target-side prompts, or partially aligned supervision, creating a mismatch between training and inference. Dubbed speech provides a promising starting point because it naturally exhibits substantial temporal and prosodic correspondence across languages, but cross-lingual speaker mismatch makes it less suitable for truly end-to-end training, where the translation pairs are parallel in every aspect. We propose a voice-conversion-based data construction framework that reduces this mismatch and turns dubbed speech into training pairs better tailored for direct expressive S2ST. To validate our data-level strategy, we train a direct S2ST model that maps source speech to target speech units without target-side acoustic prompts or intermediate expressive-transfer modules. Together, the converted speech pairs and direct model provide a more train-test-consistent setup for truly end-to-end expressive S2ST.

Voice-Conversion-Based Expressive Multilingual Alignment

Voice conversion transforms dubbed target speech toward the source speaker while preserving target-language content and expressive delivery, producing supervision better matched to direct expressive S2ST training.

Direct Modeling

Direct Speech-to-Speech Translation Model

The model directly maps source speech representations into structured target codec tokens using temporal and depth decoding, avoiding target-side acoustic prompts during inference.
